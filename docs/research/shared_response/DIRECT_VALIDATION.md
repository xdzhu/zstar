The raw Jacobian and Phonopy native force-constant array differ by a

## In2Se3 Berry-mesh convergence

Halving the displacement did not remove the in-plane BEC discrepancy.
Increasing the Berry integration mesh did. The central Se site gives:

| In-plane Berry mesh | Max in-plane shared/control BEC difference (e) |
| --- | ---: |
| 22 x 22 | 0.0093 |
| 44 x 44 | 0.002268 |
| 66 x 66 | 0.0008656 |
| 88 x 88 | 0.0003673 |

These diagnostics use only the in-plane Berry rows. Their out-of-plane
Berry values are not physical slab BECs and are not used: production
normal responses come from the unchanged charge-cube integrations.
The Cartesian symmetry-forbidden transverse response decreases with
mesh refinement. Its leakage into a mixed seed is amplified by the
ratio of normal to in-plane displacement components.

Full 88 x 88 x 2 refinement: raw BEC max difference **0.001541 e**;
projected BEC max difference 0.001239 e;
frequency max difference 0.1677 cm^-1;
static relative difference 0.7987%;
degenerate-group oscillator relative difference 0.07974%.

All original SCFs, force constants, and reference electronic dielectric
data were reused. Only polarization was recalculated; refined outputs
live in separate trees. Additional measured costs (not hidden in the
baseline timing or called additional SCFs):
- shared: 5.738 core-h for the full polarization refinement, including reused 88-mesh diagnostic stages once.
- cartesian: 16.331 core-h for the full polarization refinement, including reused 88-mesh diagnostic stages once.

The refined comparison satisfies the declared investigation targets.
The approximately 1.1% shared static step sensitivity still limits an
absolute convergence claim for this soft-mode structure. Numerical
equivalence of the two ensembles is not proof of fully converged DFT.
