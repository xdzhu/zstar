# Asset provenance

This is a compact, post-processing-only example. The retained files in
`results/base/` and `results/interpolation/` were generated from the public
SnS result set at `/home/zhuxd/abacus/zstar_potential_examples/results/SnS`
and its interpolation companion. The original raw cube and upstream SCF
inputs are not redistributed. Supply an independently generated cube with
`bash run.sh --cube PATH`.
