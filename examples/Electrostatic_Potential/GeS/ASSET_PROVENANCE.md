# Asset provenance

The structure is the relaxed monolayer GeS geometry archived at
`/home/zhuxd/abacus/10.topo/PtHgX/FE-mat/MX/GeS/soc/STRU` on the authors'
calculation server. The electronic calculation used ABACUS 3.10.0 LTS, PBE,
DOJO norm-conserving pseudopotentials, 10-au DZP numerical orbitals, a 100-Ry
cutoff, and the D3 zero-damping correction. The distributed `.upf` and `.orb`
files are exact ordinary-file copies; no executable or raw cube is included.
