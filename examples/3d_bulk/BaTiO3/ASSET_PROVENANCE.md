# Asset provenance

The `run/assets/` directory contains the exact ABACUS pseudopotentials and
numerical orbitals for the PBEsol tetragonal BaTiO3 snapshot: `Ba.upf`, `Ti.upf`,
`O.upf`, and the matching `*_gga_10au_100Ry_*` orbitals. The headers retain
the original generator notices and citations. No ABACUS or PYATB executable is
redistributed.
